Frequently Asked Questions

How long does deposit take? ▾

Deposits usually reflect within 5-10 minutes. If your deposit is pending for more than 30 minutes, please submit a complaint.

How to reset my password? ▾

Go to Email Verification section and enter your registered email. You will receive a reset link within minutes.

Withdrawal pending for long? ▾

Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours. If your withdrawal is stuck, please raise a complaint with your transaction details.

How to contact support? ▾

You can use the Online Chat feature or email us at support@crickso.com. Our team responds within 1-2 hours.
